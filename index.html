SWAP: Test-Time Steerable Humanoid Whole-Body Collision Avoidance

SWAP is a hierarchical policy that lets a humanoid robot avoid obstacles with its whole body. A diffusion planner proposes many collision-aware motions, a reinforcement learning controller keeps the robot balanced, and simple scoring functions choose among the proposals at test time, so the robot can change its goal without retraining.
